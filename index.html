MERIDIAN BEVERAGES · PHASE 1 PROTOTYPE

One purchasing decision, end to end

A retailer email becomes a Dynamics 365 purchase-order draft without the buyer leaving Outlook, Teams or D365. Seven steps, about two minutes.

What is real

The guardrail engine. Stockout projection, supplier minimums, lead-time feasibility and cover policy are computed from whatever the buyer proposes — so it can refuse, and it will.

What is mocked

All data. Meridian provided none, and none was used. One product, one distribution centre, one week. Every threshold is a placeholder.

Worth trying

On step 4, change one field and continue:

  • Quantity 500 — blocked, below the supplier minimum
  • Quantity 8,000 — warns on cover, and makes you record a reason
  • Action “Increase next order” — blocked, the date is not achievable
Nothing here connects to a live system. Press Esc to close — reopen with ? in the header.
